Common UPI Scam Scenarios

Fake Payment Screenshot

A screenshot can be edited. Check your bank or UPI app for the actual transaction.

QR Code Scam

Be cautious of QR codes that ask you to enter your PIN to receive money.

Fake Customer Care

Scammers may pose as support staff and ask for sensitive information.

Unexpected Collect Request

A collect request asks you to approve a payment from your account.

OTP Scam

Never share an OTP or UPI PIN, even with someone claiming to help you.

UPI Shield is an awareness project. Always verify transactions in your official payment app.
